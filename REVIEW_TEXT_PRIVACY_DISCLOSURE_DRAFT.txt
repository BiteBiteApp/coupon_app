Proposed customer wording for a separately approved release:

When written-review screening is enabled, BiteStar sends the headline and written
notes you submit to OpenAI's Moderation service to help identify potentially
serious inappropriate content. We do not attach your account profile, email,
payment information or photographs to this text-screening request. Information
you include in your own words is part of the submitted text. Please do not include
another person's private information. Automated screening is not a reliable
personal-information detector; the Report Review option remains available.

Approved comments become public. Proposed words flagged for review remain private
and may be reviewed by an authorized BiteStar administrator. A model flag is not
proof of misconduct and does not automatically suspend your account. Honest
negative reviews, ordinary slang, sarcasm and mild profanity are allowed.

Unpublished words awaiting human review expire 30 consecutive 24-hour days after
the original submission. Words that could not be screened are available privately
for explicit retry for up to seven consecutive 24-hour days from that submission.
Retry does not restart the clock. If a retry succeeds but flags the words, the
human-review deadline is 30 days from the same original submission. Expired words
cannot be published or returned as an active draft. Rejected proposed words are
promptly removed from the current private proposal.

Physical cleanup runs asynchronously. This is not a promise of immediate erasure
from all storage, backups or device caches. Minimal non-text identity/status
records may remain to prevent duplicate processing and stale publication and to
support moderation auditing. Their final retention terms require separate review.

Holding, rejecting or expiring proposed words does not erase your valid numerical
rating or earlier approved public words. Your explicit removal of words and
separate authorized moderation or account-deletion actions still apply.
