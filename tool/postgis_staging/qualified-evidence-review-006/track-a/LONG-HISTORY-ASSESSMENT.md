The500-pair case demonstrated a small F1 defect, now repaired: the reader asked for26 fully prepared SQL membership rows when25 restaurants were already ready and only one lookahead slot remained. The new cap requests only missing slots, retains refill after history/current-authority rejection, and measures exhaustion against the actual request limit. Independent tests retain the75-ID challenge and exact25-card result. This change may increase the number of small membership calls across a current-authority-rejected prefix; it is not a universal latency improvement.

Before that final cap, corrected unbatched native completion was10.18s/4.58s for the first/later25-card pages; batched completion was10.56s/5.29s. Both required26 identical challenge sizes,435 candidate occurrences and360 unavailable answers, with no challenge above54 IDs. Batched requests hit14 bounded membership checkpoints, spending2.25s in those calls plus2.8s of report-probe waits. Removing waits alone would still leave about13.05s across the two pages. These are local native handler observations, not actual Flutter render or hosted tail statistics.

The known slowness must remain visible as a qualification gap for a2suseful-page claim. Retained history semantics and current-authority checks require repeated bounded work until25 useful cards and lookahead are established. The evidence does not prove that all observed latency is necessary, nor isolate SQL versus Firestore authority costs sufficiently for another change. No further clear small source defect was demonstrated by this scoped assessment.

The smallest warranted next step is the parent-owned same-fixture rerun on the final cap/image, preserving per-page timings, every retry and exact page parity. Continued slowness should be reported as a measured retained-work limitation. A future hosting test may quantify deployment effects; it cannot be substituted for this already observed local case. Further optimization requires a measured bottleneck and must retain privacy, exact recovery, full useful pages and current authority. No budget increase or protocol redesign is justified by this observation alone.
