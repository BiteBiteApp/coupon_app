PROPOSED ADDITION

When you submit a customer photograph for BiteScore, BiteStar sends the
photograph to Google Cloud Vision to help screen for inappropriate content.
Authorized BiteStar administrators may also examine submitted photographs.
Approved photographs are published through the existing BiteStar photo
features. Screening is not a guarantee that every inappropriate photograph
will be detected.

Photographs awaiting an administrator's decision remain private for a review
period of up to 30 days from submission. At the end of that period, an
unapproved photograph expires, can no longer be published, and is scheduled
for deletion. Rejected unpublished photographs are scheduled for prompt
deletion. Deletion processing can take additional time, for example during
a service interruption; these photographs remain unavailable to the public.

If the upload succeeds but automated screening cannot finish, the photograph
remains private and follows [INSERT THE SEPARATELY APPROVED RETRY AND DELETION
PERIOD]. Minimal security, moderation and deletion-status records may remain
for [INSERT THE FINAL APPROVED NON-IMAGE RETENTION RULE], subject to BiteStar's
applicable account-deletion policy.
