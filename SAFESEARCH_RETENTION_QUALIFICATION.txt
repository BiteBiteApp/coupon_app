IMPLEMENTED PHOTO LIFECYCLE

Pending Admin review: expiresAt is 30 days after the original trusted server
createdAt. Customer retries and Admin actions cannot extend this deadline.
At or after expiration, server approval/publication is denied even before
maintenance runs. The queue reports expired content as unavailable for a
decision. A transaction marks it expired before any deletion work starts.

Rejection: the authoritative rejection is committed first. With cleanup enabled,
the callable promptly attempts physical deletion of the exact unpublished
object. No source photo, photo count, primary selection or contribution point
is created. A lost response or repeat rejection cannot reverse the decision.

Failed upload: an unconfirmed upload waits out the existing 120-second scan
lease before cleanup, so an interrupted put is not mistaken for permanently
absent bytes. No bytes means a verified absent disposition. A partial/lost-ack
object must match the immutable identity and upload ownership token before
its exact generation can be pinned and removed. A late finalized object
re-arms a previously completed absent check; duplicates are deduplicated.
The finalize revision prevents an in-progress absent probe from hiding an
object that arrived during that probe.

After physical cleanup is confirmed, an explicit failed-upload retry may reuse
the same immutable photo identity and ownership token, subject to the original
deadline, backoff, two-attempt maximum and admission limits. It clears obsolete
cleanup lease/generation fields. Old upload attempts cannot call the provider
or publish under the newer attempt. Rejected/expired photos cannot be revived.

Stored bytes but failed screening: remain private, with failurePhase=screening.
Seven days is implemented as a configurable SOURCE CANDIDATE. Final owner
approval is outstanding. New admissions require the new retention-policy
version, explicit failed-screening approval, a valid 1-30-day value and enabled
cleanup in addition to the existing runtime gates. A bare old retentionApproved
flag is insufficient. Retry preserves the original deadline and all cost
limits. A crashed stored scan becomes failed without an automatic Vision call.

CLEANUP SAFETY AND RECOVERY

Exact identity includes the owner, dish/restaurant/review/mode, MIME type,
SHA-256, deterministic path/id, per-record upload token and Storage ownership
metadata. Decimal generations remain strings, including values beyond JS
safe integer precision. Deletion sends both the exact generation and an
ifGenerationMatch precondition. It verifies absence afterwards, including
checking for a replacement current object. Changed identity/generation is
retained for attention, never guessed or deleted as a substitute.

Any existing public photo source, including a globally removed source,
excludes retention cleanup. Approved photos are not aged out. Stale due
markers on excluded sources are cleared so they cannot block later batches.
Deletion of a screening record during account deletion is never reversed by
a late cleanup result or finalize event. Existing account-deletion namespace,
finalize recovery, account identity and posting fences remain in place.

The scheduled worker runs every 15 minutes, at most 25 indexed due records
per invocation, maxInstances=1/concurrency=1. Only one record lease may act at
a time; crashes become eligible after 120 seconds. Failures retry with bounded
5-minute exponential backoff, at most eight automatic attempts. Exhaustion
or identity conflict remains visible in Admin as needing attention. An
authorized explicit Retry deletion starts a new bounded recovery sequence;
identity checks remain mandatory. Cleanup never calls Vision.

cleanupEnabled=false stops new physical cleanup claims and new scan admissions.
It does not cancel an already issued Storage request. Rejection still commits
a private terminal decision; cleanup waits for re-enablement. Keep this gate
enabled during an ordinary screening outage so retention continues. Use
enabled=false, required=true to stop scans/in-flight automatic publication
without reopening legacy upload paths. Administrative approval of valid
unexpired existing content remains an intentional separate action.

Publication eligibility ends at the exact deadline. Physical deletion is
asynchronous: cadence, due-page backlog, outages and Admin attention can add
time. No claim of instantaneous deletion, all-backup/cache erasure or a hard
30-day physical Storage deletion guarantee is made. Owner operations must
monitor overdue/attention cases before publicly promising retention timing.

Minimal private status/identity/deduplication records are retained under the
existing account-deletion lifecycle. Completed cleanup removes the likelihood
payload. No independent indefinite evidence-retention policy is invented.
The final non-image retention schedule remains an owner/legal release item;
do not purge late-upload/idempotency fences without a separately reviewed rule.

ADMIN AND CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

The existing private 25-item Admin queue shows dish/restaurant names, status,
server-derived age, UTC expiration, screening likelihoods when applicable and
cleanup progress. Expired/terminal records have no valid Approve/Reject action
or preview. Missing/undecodable active previews cannot be approved; they can
be rejected. Failed deletion exposes Retry deletion to authorized Admins.
An absent generation is represented consistently as null so failed uploads
can recover through that queue. Completed deletions leave the active queue.
Decision feedback distinguishes confirmed absent bytes from pending deletion.
Customer messages distinguish expired content and upload failure; existing
account-switch checks and sensitive overlay retirement are preserved.
