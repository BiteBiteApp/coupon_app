APPROVED POLICY / EXACT DEADLINES
Policy openai-clean-or-human-v1 and initial provider omni-moderation-latest are
approved. Valid clean results may publish; valid flagged results stay private
for authorized Admin review. Negative criticism, sarcasm, slang and mild profanity
are not locally prohibited. There is no blacklist, invented numeric category
threshold, model-only rejection, ban or posting restriction.

Versioned retention contract: review-text-retention-v2.
- Pending human review: original trusted submission + 2,592,000 seconds
  (30 consecutive 24-hour days).
- Failed, unavailable, paused or admission-denied screening: original submission
  + 604,800 seconds (seven consecutive 24-hour days).
- Screening in flight uses the same seven-day ceiling and existing 20-second
  attempt lease. An abandoned lease becomes a truthful failed-screening status.
- Explicit retry within seven days preserves original time/version. A valid
  flagged retry changes expiresAt to ORIGINAL + 30 days; a clean retry publishes
  only if publication fences still permit it. No retry restarts either clock.
- A valid flagged completion keeps its 30-day human-review deadline even if a
  posting restriction arises during screening. That restriction still prevents
  Admin approval and clean publication. Invalid/paused processing stays failed.
- At the applicable boundary, expired words cannot publish or be returned by
  author/Admin reads, regardless of scheduler delay. Current clocks are sampled
  after awaited reads; delayed completions and decisions cannot cross the check.
- Rejection strips headline/notes immediately in the decision transaction.
  Approval also strips the private raw copy. Terminal records have no expiresAt,
  so completed work cannot occupy the raw-text expiry queue.

Rating transactions and their aggregate calculations remain unchanged. Holding,
rejecting, failing or expiring an edit preserves legitimate ratings, helpful
votes, photos, points and earlier approved public words. Explicit blank-field
removal and separate authorized moderation/deletion actions still apply.
Stale attempts, superseded edits, changed dish/restaurant identity, merge and
operation locks, account deletion and same-ID withdrawal/recreation remain
fenced. No historical published-word expiration was added.

TTL / RECOVERY / MINIMAL RECEIPT
The existing 25-row worker runs every 15 minutes when separately activated. It
rechecks current expiry transactionally, strips due raw words and leaves an
expired status; duplicate runs and crash recovery are safe. Terminal rows no
longer crowd later work. The existing source TTL candidate on
private_bitescore_review_text.expiresAt will provide the asynchronous backstop
once separately deployed and verified. No index or scheduler was added here.

TTL can delete the WHOLE proposal before the worker. A narrow private non-text
receipt therefore survives separately in private_bitescore_review_text_receipts.
It stores only review/dish/restaurant/user identity, version, content digest,
original createdAt, retention policy version and status. It has no headline,
notes or provider signals. Same-content resubmission after TTL remains expired;
it cannot silently obtain a new deadline. Different proposed words create a new
version. Withdrawal, explicit text removal and account deletion clean receipts;
delayed withdrawal events cannot delete a matching current replacement. Rules
deny every client role direct access. Account-deletion cleanup appends a family
without shifting existing persisted stage indexes. Final non-image metadata
retention remains a separate owner/legal item; no general lifetime was invented.

At full batches the supplemental worker handles at most 100 rows/hour or 2,400
rows/day. Together with TTL this is proportionate for a bounded beta, conditional
on verifying TTL and observing actual arrivals/backlog before activation. It is
not a guarantee for unspecified production load. Release operations must watch
existing scheduler failures, repeatedly saturated batches, oldest overdue raw
proposal and TTL deletion/lag metrics using existing monitoring/manual checks.
Failures must be investigated and recovered through the same bounded worker;
larger capacity requires evidence and separate approval, not another cleanup job.

Firestore documents TTL deletion as asynchronous, typically within 24 hours, and
uses the latest TTL field. This is not a deletion-time SLA or immediate erasure
from backups/internal storage/device caches. No production TTL run was qualified.
Local tests simulate whole-document TTL deletion and exercise worker recovery.
