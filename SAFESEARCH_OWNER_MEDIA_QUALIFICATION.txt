IDENTITY, PUBLICATION AND COST CONTRACT
Server-created objects use the EXISTING bucket and hashed owner namespace:
  bitescore_user_uploads/<hashed-owner-key>/owner_images/screened_bso_<hash>.jpg
The owner key uses the existing bitestar.dish-upload.v1 derivation. No raw UID
is exposed in that public object path. No public download token is issued.
Objects are immutable and bound to validated bytes, MIME/size/content hash,
Storage generation, live authenticated uploader lifetime, exact source creation
time, family and target incarnation. Clients cannot forge scan/publication
state, supply arbitrary public URLs, or write/list/delete these private bytes.
Private previews recheck authority and immutable generation. Public bytes require
both approval and the exact saved reference; mere Admin approval is insufficient.

The existing provider adapter and conservative-hold-v1 policy are reused:
adult/violence/racy all VERY_UNLIKELY or UNLIKELY permits auto-approval; otherwise
human review. Medical/spoof alone does not reject. No AI-only rejection rule.
One existing private_bitescore_screening_usage/YYYY-MM pool admits at most
1,000 attempts per UTC month SHARED by BiteScore and BiteSaver. Existing global
concurrency, per-user admission, idempotency, explicit retry/backoff and pause
limits are shared. Cleanup, triggers, galleries, status reads and Admin previews
do not invoke Vision. Retries require explicit admission and retain the original
deadline/attempt limit; provider failure fails closed.

polling scheduler. Small private decision/cleanup records remain distinct from
